<p>Video Editing is a free and open-source video editor for Android focused on simplicity, performance, and privacy. Trim, edit, and export watermark-free videos entirely on your device, with no accounts and no internet access.</p>

<p><b>Note:</b> Video Editing is currently in beta. You may encounter bugs, instability, or incomplete features as development continues.</p>

<p><b>Editing</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Trim:</b> Remove unwanted parts from the beginning or end of a clip with a real-time timeline control.</li>
<li><b>Merge:</b> Combine multiple clips into one sequence and rearrange them by dragging.</li>
<li><b>Speed:</b> Speed up or slow down clips with a fine-grained slider.</li>
<li><b>Reverse:</b> Play clips backwards.</li>
<li><b>Crop:</b> Crop to custom aspect ratios.</li>
<li><b>Transitions:</b> Add transitions with animated previews.</li>
<li><b>Adjust &amp; Filters:</b> Change brightness, contrast, saturation, and apply color filters.</li>
<li><b>Canvas Background:</b> Blurred or solid background for mismatched aspect ratios.</li>
<li><b>Freehand Drawing:</b> Draw on top of the video with custom brush color and stroke.</li>
<li><b>Snapshots:</b> Save high-quality frame grabs from the preview.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Overlays</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Overlays:</b> Add text, stickers, images, GIFs, and video overlays with optional looping.</li>
<li><b>Keyframes:</b> Animate overlays across the screen.</li>
<li><b>Masking:</b> Apply rectangle, ellipse, split, shutter, heart, and star masks.</li>
<li><b>Chroma Key:</b> Remove green-screen backgrounds from overlays.</li>
<li><b>Layer Management:</b> Reorder overlay layers.</li>
<li><b>Subtitles:</b> Import .srt subtitle files and reposition captions in the preview.</li>
<li><b>Custom Fonts:</b> Import .ttf and .otf fonts for text overlays.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Audio</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Audio:</b> Import music, record voice-overs, apply ducking and fades, boost volume up to 200%, or mute original audio.</li>
<li><b>Audio Export:</b> Export the full audio mix as a standalone MP3 file.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Workflow</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Projects:</b> Save and reopen non-destructive projects as .lcprj files.</li>
<li><b>Proxy Generation:</b> Smooth editing of heavy footage with background proxies.</li>
<li><b>Screen Recording:</b> Record the screen from the app or a Quick Settings tile.</li>
<li><b>Fullscreen Preview:</b> Expanded timeline view and overlay controls.</li>
<li><b>Timeline Tools:</b> Snapping, overlay duplication, and freeze frames.</li>
<li><b>Hardware Acceleration:</b> Fast exports using hardware encoders, with automatic software fallback.</li>
</ul>
